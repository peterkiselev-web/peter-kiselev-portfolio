We take the repetitive work off your staff.

K & Co is an advisory firm in London and Dubai, founded by Peter Kiselev. We build AI systems for the phone nobody has time to answer and the hundred small handoffs that still happen over email. The team behind it is large and hand-picked, and we'd rather stay with a client for years than leave after one project.

Founded by Peter Kiselev · London · Dubai

  1. Before

    Most companies run on habit.

    Somebody copies figures from one system into another because that's how it was done in 2019. It mostly works. Nobody wrote it down.

  2. Mapping

    We draw what actually happens.

    Our first weeks go to the people who do the work, since they're the only ones who know how it really runs. It's rarely what the org chart says.

  3. After

    The routine runs itself.

    Repeat steps happen on their own and leave a record. Anything that needs judgement still goes to a person, and you decide which steps those are.

One line from the first email to the final report, with a person wherever judgement is needed.

The terms we keep

They're written into every contract we sign.

  1. We don't name clients.

    Not on this site, and not at dinner either.

    Names

  2. Your data stays where it is.

    Systems run in your cloud accounts or on hardware you control. We don't train models on your data, and we don't keep copies after handover.

    Data

  3. The NDA comes first.

    We'll send ours before the first real conversation. Or we'll sign yours.

    NDA

  4. We have nothing to sell you.

    We don't resell software and we don't take commissions from vendors. If we recommend a tool, it's because it suits you.

    Fees

  5. Hand-picked, all of them.

    The team is larger than our profile suggests, and Peter Kiselev chose every person on it himself.

    Team
