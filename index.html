· The firm

K & Co is an advisory firm in London and Dubai. We build AI systems that take the repetitive work off your staff: the phone nobody has time to answer, say, or the hundred small handoffs that still happen over email. Behind the work is a large team, every one of them hand-picked, and we'd rather stay with a client for years than leave after one project.

Most companies run on habit.

· Discretion

The terms we keep

They're written into every contract we sign.

  1. 01

    We don't name clients.

    Not on this site, and not at dinner either.

  2. 02

    Your data stays where it is.

    Systems run in your cloud accounts or on hardware you control. We don't train models on your data, and we don't keep copies after handover.

  3. 03

    The NDA comes first.

    We'll send ours before the first real conversation. Or we'll sign yours.

  4. 04

    We have nothing to sell you.

    We don't resell software and we don't take commissions from vendors. If we recommend a tool, it's because it suits you.

  5. 05

    Hand-picked, all of them.

    The team is larger than our profile suggests, and Peter Kiselev chose every person on it himself.
